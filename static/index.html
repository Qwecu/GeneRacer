Hotseat race

Each player picks one of their own cars. Other players join with their own password. Players drive one after another on the same keyboard. Finishers rank by time; everyone else by how far they got. Earlier runs appear as ghosts.

The 100 best time-trial runs on each track, shared by all players. The top 10 keep their ghosts. Each player and car can hold one place; a new run replaces it only if it is better. Finishers rank by time, everyone else by distance.
