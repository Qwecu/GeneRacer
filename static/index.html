Garage

Your cars may not be very good, but they are all useless in their own, unique way. You can breed cars to make baby cars that inherit their parents' genes.

You can feel frustrated when your cars don't perform well. It is normal. Remember that each car has its own (strengths and) weaknesses.

Hotseat race

Each player picks one of their own cars. Other players join with their own password. Players drive one after another on the same keyboard. Finishers rank by time; everyone else by how far they got. Earlier runs appear as ghosts.

Info

Breeding and mutations

Each parent passes one of its two chromosomes to the child, built by single-point crossover: genes that sit next to each other on the chromosome tend to be inherited together. Randomly generated and starter cars have no mutations, their genes are simply random.

After that, every allele the child receives gets two separate chances to mutate, each 2.5%:

  • Value mutation: the allele's value (0-255) shifts by a random amount of at most 40, up or down.
  • Dominance mutation: the allele's dominance is replaced by a new random number from 0 to 255. This can change which allele is expressed.

A child has about 82 alleles, so it gets roughly 4 mutations on average. Most are small value shifts. The game does not announce them: compare the child's genotype table with its parents' to spot one.

Genes

41 loci, each expressed in one of four ways: dominant (the allele with higher dominance wins), avg (additive), low (the weaker allele wins), high (one strong allele is enough).
